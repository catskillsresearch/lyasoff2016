Mathematical English for `Foliation.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

Proof-path note: the selected Comparator theorems below are **conditional rearrangements**. Each takes the paper’s analytic input as a hypothesis (`SatisfiesTheorem41PDE`, `SatisfiesStrikingIdentity`, `SatisfiesFoliationODE`, or `TransformedMassIdentity` / `HasLaplaceReduction`) and discharges the algebraic/change-of-variables step. They do **not** establish those inputs for the joint/conditional density of exponential Brownian motion.

**Definition** (`SatisfiesTheorem41PDE`). Hypothesis: the first-order transport PDE along `V` holds for a candidate conditional density.

**Theorem** (`theorem_4_1_pde`). Conditional form of p. 1071–1073, Theorem 4.1 (PDE part): if `SatisfiesTheorem41PDE`, then the written PDE identity holds at every `x > 0`.

Proof idea: unfold the hypothesis (`htransport`).

**Definition** (`SatisfiesStrikingIdentity`). Hypothesis: the Donati-Martin–Matsumoto–Yor reciprocal-moment cases (PAPER NOTE: printed `∫₀ᵗ` should be `∫₀^∞ x⁻¹ φ(x|·) dx`).

**Theorem** (`theorem_4_1_striking`). Conditional form of p. 1073, Theorem 4.1 identity (4.4): if `SatisfiesStrikingIdentity`, then the `y = 0` / `y ≠ 0` cases of the striking identity hold.

Proof idea: case-split on `y = 0` and apply the two conjuncts of the hypothesis.

**Theorem** (`hasDerivAt_ode_solution`). Derivative of `exp(−u/2 − (4/x) cosh(u/2))`. PAPER NOTE: printed sinh sign is wrong; the consistent sign is minus.

Proof idea: chain rule / `HasDerivAt` for the linear and hyperbolic pieces, then the exponential.

**Definition** (`SatisfiesFoliationODE`). Hypothesis: the integrated ODE (4.5) with integration constant fixed at `u = 0`.

**Theorem** (`formula_4_6`). Conditional form of p. 1075–1076, formula (4.6): if `SatisfiesFoliationODE`, then the displayed `T_{t,y}` rearrangement from the `y = 0` slice holds.

Proof idea: instantiate the ODE at a rescaled base point and simplify the exponential/cosh algebra.

**Definition** (`TransformedMassIdentity`). Hypothesis: the transformed mass identity (4.7) at the zero endpoint.
