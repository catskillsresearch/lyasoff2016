**Lyasoff (2016), p. 1091, closed forms for S**

S_{0,p}(A) = ∫₀^∞ e^{-A/x} x^{1-p} dx = A^{-(p-2)} Γ(p-2) for p ≥ 3.


**Lyasoff (2016), p. 1091, closed form for S_{k,p}**

after the substitution v = A/x,
   S_{k,p}(A) = k A^{-(p-1)} Γ(p-1, A/k) − A^{-(p-2)} Γ(p-2, A/k)
                − k A^{-(p-1)} Γ(p-1) + A^{-(p-2)} Γ(p-2).
