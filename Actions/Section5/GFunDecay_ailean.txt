**Lemma** (`cerf_zero`). on a horizontal line the two `cerf` terms of each pair in (5.8) tend to `+1` and `−1` respectively, at Gaussian rate, so the constants cancel and |g_{t,3}(u − ia)| ≤ C e^{−u²/t}, |g_{t,4}(u − ia)| ≤ C cosh u · e^{−u²/t}. Together with |g_{t,2}(u − ia)| = e^{2a²/t} e^{−2u²/t}/(4π) this makes `cosh u · |g_{t,p}(u − ia)|` integrable, since ∫ e^{−c u²} cosh² u du < ∞. (The bounds `ContourRec.norm_gFun_three_line_le`, `…four_line_le` only give `C`, `C cosh u`, which is enough for the contour equation but not for Fubini at a = π/2.)
