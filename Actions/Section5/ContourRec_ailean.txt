**Lemma** (`cerf_bdd_line`). the functions g_{t,p} of (5.8) satisfy g_{t,3}' = −4 sinh · g_{t,2} and g_{t,4}' = −4 sinh · g_{t,3} (this is what makes the residue recipe (5.7) produce (5.5)). Along the lower edge z = u − ia, with D = cosh z − cosh y, d/du [ g_{t,p}(z) / D^{p−2} ] = −4 g_{t,p−1}(z) sinh z / D^{p−2} − (p−2) g_{t,p}(z) sinh z / D^{p−1}, and g_{t,p}(z) / D^{p−2} → 0 as u → ±∞ (cerf is bounded on horizontal lines and |D| ≥ cosh u when cos a ≤ 0). Integrating over ℝ gives ∫ (5.3)-integrand for p = −(4/(p−2)) ∫ (5.3)-integrand for p−1, which is the recursion (−4)^{p−2}/(p−2)! of the right-hand side of (5.3).
