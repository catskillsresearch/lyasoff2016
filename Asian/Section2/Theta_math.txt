**Lyasoff (2016), p. 1062, θ(r,t) in (1.1)**

$$
\theta(r,t)=\frac{r}{\sqrt{2\pi^3 t}}\,e^{\frac{\pi^2}{2t}}\int_0^\infty e^{-\frac{u^2}{2t}}\,e^{-r\cosh u}\,\sinh u\,\sin\frac{\pi u}{t}\,\mathrm{d}u.
$$
