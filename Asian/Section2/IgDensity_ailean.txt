Mathematical English for `IgDensity.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

**Definition** (`igDensity`). Inverse-Gamma density written as the law of `1/Y` for `Y ~ Gamma(β, rate a)`. `igDensity β a x = invGammaDensity (β+1) a x` on `(0,∞)`.

**Theorem** (`integral_Ioi`). The change of variables `x = 1/y` turns the Inverse-Gamma density into a Gamma density.

**Definition** (`igMeasure`). The law with density `igDensity β a`.
