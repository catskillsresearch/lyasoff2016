Mathematical English for `Line.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

**Definition** (`line`). the flow is u ↦ (x e^{σ²u/2}, y + σ u). PAPER NOTE: printed second coordinate (y+u)σ is wrong; flow of σ∂ᵧ is y+σu.

**Theorem** (`is_integral_curve`). that curve is an integral curve of the vector field V.

**Theorem** (`foliation`). every point (x,y) in ℝ₊₊×ℝ lies on the line through (x e^{-σy/2}, 0).
