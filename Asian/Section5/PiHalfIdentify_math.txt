**Lyasoff (2016), p. 1086, (5.10a), (5.10b)**

which are equivalent to the formulae, for the unconditional joint density,

$$
\phi_{t,\sigma}(x, y) = \frac{16}{\sigma^{2p-2} x^p \sqrt{2\pi t}}\, e^{\frac{1}{2}\sigma y(p-1) - \frac{2}{\sigma^2 x}(e^{\sigma y} + 1)} \times \int_0^{\infty} \cosh u \, \mathfrak{R}\!\left(i\, e^{-i \frac{4}{\sigma^2 x} e^{\sigma y/2} \sinh u}\, g_{\sigma^2 t,p}(u - i\pi/2)\right) du, \qquad\text{(5.10a)}
$$

$$
\phi_{t,\sigma}(x, y) = \frac{16}{\sigma^{2p-2} x^p \sqrt{2\pi t}}\, e^{\frac{1}{2}\sigma y(p-1) - \frac{2}{\sigma^2 x}(e^{\sigma y} + 1)} \times \int_0^{\infty} \sinh u \, \mathfrak{R}\!\left(e^{-\frac{4}{\sigma^2 x} e^{\sigma y/2} \cosh u}\, g_{\sigma^2 t,p}(u - i\pi)\right) du. \qquad\text{(5.10b)}
$$
