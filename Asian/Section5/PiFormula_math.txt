**Lyasoff (2016), pp. 1085–1086, (5.9b), (5.10b)**

$$
\phi_{t,\sigma}(x \mid y) = \frac{16}{\sigma^{2p-2} x^p}\, e^{\frac{1}{2}\sigma y(p-1) + \frac{y^2}{2t} - \frac{2}{\sigma^2 x}(e^{\sigma y} + 1)} \times \int_0^{\infty} \sinh u \, \mathfrak{R}\!\left(e^{-\frac{4}{\sigma^2 x} e^{\sigma y/2} \cosh u}\, g_{\sigma^2 t,p}(u - i\pi)\right) du. \qquad\text{(5.9b)}
$$

$$
\phi_{t,\sigma}(x, y) = \frac{16}{\sigma^{2p-2} x^p \sqrt{2\pi t}}\, e^{\frac{1}{2}\sigma y(p-1) - \frac{2}{\sigma^2 x}(e^{\sigma y} + 1)} \times \int_0^{\infty} \sinh u \, \mathfrak{R}\!\left(e^{-\frac{4}{\sigma^2 x} e^{\sigma y/2} \cosh u}\, g_{\sigma^2 t,p}(u - i\pi)\right) du. \qquad\text{(5.10b)}
$$
