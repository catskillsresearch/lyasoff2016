Mathematical English for `StopPath.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

**Definition** (`stopPath`). The path stopped at time `t`.

**Theorem** (`integral_eq`). On `[0, t]` the stopped path agrees with the path, so the time integral up to `t` of the stopped path only sees the values `y` of the path.

Supporting declarations without a docstring: `measurable`.
