Mathematical English for `Setting.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

**Abbreviation** (`IsBM`). a Brownian motion starts at 0, has continuous paths, and Gaussian independent increments. We use Mathlib's `IsBrownianReal` (time indexed by `ℝ≥0`).

**Definition** (`expInt`). the integral of exponential Brownian motion with drift up to time t.

**Definition** (`jointLaw`). the law of the pair (∫₀ᵗ e^{σW_s+νs} ds, W_t).

**Definition** (`IsJointDensity`). φ is a Lebesgue density of that joint law.

**Definition** (`IsCondDensity`). the conditional density φ(x|y) of the integral given W_t = y, related by φ(x,y) = gaussianPDF(y) · φ(x|y).

**Definition** (`pd`). mixed partial ∂^{m+n}/∂x^m ∂y^n.

**Definition** (`invGammaDensity`). inverse-Gamma shape densities a^{p-1}/Γ(p-1) · x^{-p} e^{-a/x}.

**Theorem** (`time_reversal`). time-reversibility in law for integral functionals of BM: (∫ V_i(s,W_s) ds, W_t) has the same law as (∫ V_i(t-s, W_t-W_s) ds, W_t).
