The bent-path identity for the Asian integrand. It extends `F` to complex `u` (`FfunC`), shows that it is entire, agrees with `F` on the reals, and has the needed conjugate symmetry. If the homotopy to the sech-bent contour `u + iθ₀ sech u` is dominated (`BendDominated`), then `∫₀^∞ ℜ F du` equals half the real part of the bent-path integral. That domination is an assumption here and is not yet proved.
