Mathematical English for `Dufresne.lean` (Lean names in typewriter).

**Definition** (`dufresne`). ψ(x) = (2/σ²)^β / Γ(β) · x^{-β-1} e^{-2/(σ² x)} with β = −2ν/σ² on x > 0 (and 0 for x ≤ 0, since the infinite-horizon integral is a.s. positive).

**Theorem** (`zero_flux`). Dufresne’s ψ satisfies (σ²/2)(x²ψ)' − (1+(ν+σ²/2)x)ψ = 0.

**Theorem** (`integral_Ioi`). Dufresne's ψ integrates to 1 over (0, ∞) (substitution u = 1/x reduces it to the Gamma integral).

**Theorem** (`scaling`). The inverse-gamma density has the Brownian-scaling covariance required by `inftyLaw_scaling`.

**Theorem** (`measurable`). The nonnegative density used by `dufresne_theorem` is measurable.

**Theorem** (`theorem_of_sigma_one`). Brownian scaling reduces Dufresne's theorem at arbitrary positive volatility to its unit-volatility case.

**Theorem** (`eq_igDensity_one`). the infinite-horizon integral has Dufresne’s density (supported on x > 0). Available pieces: `dufresne_zero_flux`, `dufresne_nonneg`, `integral_dufresne_Ioi`, `Z_stationary`.

**Theorem** (`dufresne_zero_flux`). Board-facing alias of `dufresne.zero_flux`.

**Theorem** (`zero_flux_logDeriv`). The logarithmic derivative implied by the zero-flux ODE: `(x² ψ)' / (x² ψ) = (2/σ²)(x⁻² + (ν + σ²/2) x⁻¹)` wherever `ψ x ≠ 0`.

Supporting declarations without a docstring: `nonneg`.
