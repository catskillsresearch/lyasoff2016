§4 Foliation of the joint law (pp. 1071–1079). The Comparator theorems `theorem_4_1_pde`, `theorem_4_1_striking`, `formula_4_6`, `prop_4_4`, and `theorem_4_5` prove the paper's identities from its analytic inputs: the transport PDE, the ODE along each line, the mass identity (4.7), and the Laplace reduction. The four `cond_density_satisfies_*` theorems then show that the Brownian-scaled Yor conditional density satisfies each input, so the identities hold for an actual conditional density. The module also proves uniqueness of the inverse-argument Laplace transform and the first negative moments at `y = 0`.
