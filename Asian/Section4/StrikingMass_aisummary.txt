The analysis behind the striking identity (4.4). It defines the Laplace transform in `1/x` of a slice and the exponent `a(z) = (4/σ²)(cosh(σz/2) − 1)` of the mass identity (4.7). Differentiating (4.7) under the integral sign gives the weighted reciprocal moments for `z ≠ 0`, and monotone convergence then gives `∫ x⁻¹ φ(x | 0) dx = 1/t`.
